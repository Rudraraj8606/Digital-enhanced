## 1. What the Relay Server Does

The relay server is a passive message conduit. It temporarily routes
circuit data between users in the same collaboration room. It does not
store, inspect, monitor, or log the content of any messages transmitted
through it. Rooms and their contents are deleted automatically after
2 hours of inactivity or when all members disconnect.
